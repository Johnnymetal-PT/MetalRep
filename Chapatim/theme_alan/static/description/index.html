Why Choose Theme Alan?

Theme Alan is a feature-rich Odoo eCommerce theme built for businesses that want more than a standard storefront. Designed from the ground up for both B2C and B2B operations, it delivers advanced functionality, deep customization, and a conversion-focused shopping experience — all without requiring developer intervention for day-to-day changes.

1. Designed Mobile-First, Responsive by Default
Theme Alan is built with a mobile-first approach, ensuring your store looks and performs flawlessly on smartphones, tablets, and desktops. With 10+ flexible header layouts and 10+ customizable footer styles, you can shape the structure of your site to match your brand — not the other way around.

2. Advanced eCommerce Capabilities
Go beyond a basic product listing with features built to increase engagement and sales: Advanced Smart Search with real-time suggestions, Dynamic Hotspots for interactive shoppable images, AI-powered shopping assistance, AJAX-based shop loading for uninterrupted browsing, Quick View, Dynamic Pricing & Discounts, Barcode Integration on product pages, and an optimized mini cart with promo code support.

3. Built for B2B — Not Just B2C
Theme Alan includes a dedicated B2B experience: custom registration flows with approval controls, wholesale pricing display, bulk ordering support, and catalog visibility controls. Whether you run a trade store or a mixed B2B/B2C operation, Alan handles it natively.

4. Deep Customization Without Code
The Mega Menu Builder and Snippet Builder let you design complex navigation menus and page sections visually. Customize colors, fonts, layouts, and product card styles through the theme configurator. For advanced requirements, the clean codebase gives developers full flexibility to extend further.

5. A Trusted, Actively Maintained Theme
Available from Odoo v12 through v19, Theme Alan has been continuously developed and updated by Atharva System. With dedicated support at support@atharvasystem.com and documentation included in the purchased module, you're backed by an experienced team throughout your project.

Is demo data included with the theme?

No, demo data is not included. The live preview is for showcase purposes only. Documentation is available for the purchased module to help you recreate similar layouts quickly.

What is the upgrade policy between Odoo versions?

Each major Odoo version requires a separate purchase. For example, a license for v17 does not cover v19. Within the same major version, bug fixes and minor updates are provided at no additional cost. This follows the standard Odoo Apps licensing policy.

Do you offer customization services?

Yes, we offer professional customization services including custom functionality, design changes, and third-party module integrations. Contact us at support@atharvasystem.com or visit www.atharvasystem.com to discuss your requirements.

How fast is support response time?

We respond to all support requests within 24 business hours. Raise a ticket via the Support Page on Odoo Apps, or email us at support@atharvasystem.com. Please use the support@atharvasystem.com email for bug reports and pre-sales queries.

Can the theme be used on Odoo.sh and On-Premise?

Yes, Theme Alan is fully compatible with both deployment options — Odoo.sh (cloud platform) and On-Premise installations — and works seamlessly across all of them.

Is the theme compatible with both Community and Enterprise?

Yes, Theme Alan v19 is designed to work with both Odoo v19 Community and Enterprise editions. All features are fully compatible with both editions.

Can I use Theme Alan on multiple databases?

No. One license is valid for one Odoo database only. A separate license must be purchased for each additional project or database.

Can I resell or redistribute the theme?

No. Redistribution or reselling is strictly prohibited under the OPL-1 license terms.

Is Theme Alan compatible with Odoo's multi-website feature?

Yes, Theme Alan supports multi-website setups. You can run multiple websites from a single Odoo instance, each with its own independent configuration.
